Getting around

  • The top navigation bar moves between the five main sections: Home, About, Projects, Reflection, and Resume & Contact. Previous / Next links at the bottom of each section also step through them in order.
  • Inside Projects, the left sidebar switches between Department Weeks, Mock Product, Capstone, Bonus Courses, and Shadow Days. Breadcrumbs at the top of each page show exactly where you are.
  • Each department week has Previous / Next links at the bottom to move through all seven in order.

About the data shown

SEA (Social Engagement Accelerator) is a fictional concept product built for this internship, not a real Evisions product. Screenshots in the Department Weeks section come from Argos’ standard training database, a long-running fictional dataset Evisions uses to teach new users how the software works. None of it reflects real students, employees, or institutions.
